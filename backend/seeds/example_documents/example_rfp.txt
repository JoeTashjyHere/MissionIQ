SECTION L — INSTRUCTIONS TO OFFERORS
─────────────────────────────────────
L.1 General. This solicitation is a competitive 8(a) procurement issued under
FAR Part 15. Proposals shall be submitted electronically via PIEE no later
than the response due date.

L.2 Proposal Volumes.
  Volume I  — Technical Approach (page limit: 40 pages)
  Volume II — Management Approach (page limit: 25 pages)
  Volume III — Past Performance (page limit: 20 pages)
  Volume IV — Price (no page limit)

L.3 Volume I — Technical Approach
L.3.1 The Contractor shall describe its approach to providing 24x7 operational
support of the DHA mission operations center, including incident response,
performance monitoring, and continuity of operations.
L.3.2 The Contractor shall describe its proposed transition-in plan, including
knowledge transfer from the incumbent, within 60 days of award.
L.3.3 The Contractor shall describe its information assurance approach,
including FedRAMP Moderate alignment and DoD IL5 controls where applicable.

L.4 Volume II — Management Approach
L.4.1 The Contractor shall identify a Program Manager with a minimum of
ten (10) years of experience supporting DoD health programs.
L.4.2 The Contractor shall describe its staffing plan to fill all key
positions within thirty (30) days of award.

L.5 Volume III — Past Performance
L.5.1 The Contractor shall submit a maximum of three (3) past performance
references for contracts of similar size, scope, and complexity performed
within the last five (5) years.

SECTION M — EVALUATION FACTORS
──────────────────────────────
M.1 Best Value Trade-Off Evaluation. The Government will award one IDIQ
contract resulting from this solicitation to the responsible Offeror whose
proposal represents the best value to the Government.

M.2 Evaluation Factors (in descending order of importance):
  Factor 1 — Technical Approach
  Factor 2 — Management Approach
  Factor 3 — Past Performance
  Factor 4 — Price

M.3 Technical Approach (Most Important). The Government will evaluate the
extent to which the proposed approach meets the requirements of Section C
(PWS), including 24x7 operations, transition-in, and information assurance.

M.4 Management Approach (Important). The Government will evaluate the
proposed Program Manager qualifications and the staffing plan.

M.5 Past Performance (Important). The Government will evaluate the relevance
and quality of submitted past performance references.

M.6 Price (Less Important). The Government will evaluate proposed price for
realism and reasonableness.

SECTION C — PERFORMANCE WORK STATEMENT (excerpt)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
C.1 Mission. The Contractor shall provide mission operations support services
to the DHA Operations Center to ensure continuity of mission-essential
functions across all DHA components.

C.2 Scope. Services include but are not limited to:
  (a) 24x7 monitoring and incident response for operational systems
  (b) Performance reporting and dashboard maintenance
  (c) Continuity of operations planning and exercises
  (d) Transition support for newly onboarded systems
  (e) Knowledge management and standard operating procedure development

C.3 Period of Performance. One (1) twelve (12) month base period plus four
(4) twelve (12) month option periods, for a total potential five-year period.

C.4 Deliverables.
  CDRL A001 — Monthly Status Report (due 10th of each month)
  CDRL A002 — Quarterly Performance Review (due 30 days after each quarter)
  CDRL A003 — Transition Plan (due 30 days after award)
  CDRL A004 — Incident Reports (within 24 hours of qualifying incidents)
  CDRL A005 — Annual Continuity of Operations Test Report

C.5 Place of Performance. Falls Church, VA, with telework as authorized by
the Contracting Officer's Representative.

C.6 Security. The Contractor shall comply with all applicable DoD information
assurance policies, including DoDI 8500.01 and NIST 800-53 Moderate
baseline. All personnel shall hold a minimum of a Public Trust clearance;
key positions require Secret.
